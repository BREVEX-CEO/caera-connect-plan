# OCADE Terms of Service — Pre-launch Draft

**Status:** Indian and target-market lawyer review required

**Last updated:** 15 August 2026
**Effective date:** not yet effective; activate only when published and accepted

## 1. Provider and eligibility

The final terms must name the verified provider, legal form, address and contact.
Until incorporation/assignment is completed, the repository records Haans
Antoni as the owner operating the CAERA CADANE brand. Users must have capacity to
contract. Age/parental-consent rules must be resolved before launch.

## 2. Service

OCADE combines local software and optional online account, Private Sync, cloud,
payment and remote-device features. Availability varies by hardware, platform,
region, store approval and plan. Source presence, tests, screenshots and
previews are not promises that a production feature is available.

## 3. Account and devices

Users must protect their account and devices and promptly report unauthorized
access. Current entitlement source allows up to **two desktop and two mobile
devices**. The final commercial schedule must define replacement, household,
business-seat, transfer and abuse rules.

## 4. Demo, cloud allowance and local access

Demo mode uses sample data and does not create a production entitlement. Current
source models **28 successful cloud requests per aligned eight-hour window**;
failed/cancelled/system-retried requests do not consume allowance, and supported
local processing remains unlimited. This is not “28 hours on, 8 hours locked.”

After subscription expiry, cloud/premium features may stop, but supported local
inference, local data access and local export remain available. Final copy must
match the checkout and production enforcement exactly.

## 5. Pricing, renewal, cancellation and refunds

No production payment integration is verified, so this draft states no price.
Before accepting money, show currency, taxes, term, auto-renewal, allowance,
device limits, cancellation steps, refund policy, trial conversion, provider
terms and statutory consumer rights at checkout. Obtain express authorization
for recurring payment. India UPI should use supported Intent/QR flows rather
than the deprecated UPI Collect flow.

Mobile digital purchases may require Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play
Billing. Any alternative billing program must be joined and implemented under
the store’s actual terms; web payment availability is not an exemption.

## 6. User content and privacy

Users retain rights in their content and grant only the permission needed to
provide the selected features, secure the service and comply with law. Users
must have authority to provide content and access any system/account used with
OCADE. The final [Privacy Notice](PRIVACY_NOTICE.md) is incorporated by reference.

## 7. AI and computer-control risks

AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete or unsafe and must be verified before
important use. Computer control may open applications, type, read screens, move
or read files and act on accounts. Users must supervise consequential actions,
respect confirmation prompts and use emergency stop when necessary. OCADE is
not professional medical, legal, tax or financial advice.

## 8. Acceptable use

Users may not break law; infringe rights; access systems without authorization;
distribute malware; evade security, entitlement or payment controls; exploit
vulnerabilities; or harm people. Restrictions on reverse engineering apply only
to the extent allowed by mandatory law and never override an applicable
third-party/open-source licence.

## 9. Availability, suspension and termination

Online services may change or be suspended for security, abuse, non-payment or
legal reasons, with notice and an appeal route where practicable. The final
terms must specify data export, account closure, cloud deletion, backup expiry
and dispute handling. Termination must not claim a right to destroy user-owned
local data outside OCADE’s control.

## 10. Intellectual property

Haans Antoni owns the proprietary repository rights to the extent established by
the documented chain of title, pending any written assignment to an incorporated
entity. Third-party components/models/assets remain under their own terms.
CAERA CADANE/OCADE trademark claims must match actual applications/registrations.

## 11. Disclaimers, liability and consumer law

Any “as is” wording and liability cap must preserve non-waivable consumer,
product, negligence, fraud, data-protection and other statutory rights. A lawyer
must set the cap and exclusions for India and every launch market; do not publish
the repository’s old blanket clause as global consumer terms.

## 12. Law, disputes and contact

Indian law and Bengaluru jurisdiction are proposed, subject to mandatory local
consumer rules. The final terms must define notice, complaint, escalation and
service-of-process methods. Intended emails are not active legal channels until
verified.

## 13. Changes

Give advance notice of material changes and obtain renewed acceptance/consent
where law requires. Keep an immutable version and acceptance record.
