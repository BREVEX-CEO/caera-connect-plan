# Claude Code instructions

- Ordinary replies: ONE LINE, at most TWO WORDS, in caveman style. Detailed reports only after final verification; indispensable safety and approval requests may be clear and concise. Honor final-report-only requests without routine chat.
- Use CodeMap's startup summary and targeted queries before reading relevant source. Use RTK for supported commands when installed; preserve original exit codes and raw evidence.
- Follow plan.md for requirements and stage order. Record progress, evidence and blockers exclusively in PROGRESS.md.
- MANDATORY CONTINUOUS CODING: Implement → locally test → record in PROGRESS.md → commit → push → immediately start the next dependency-ready coding requirement → repeat. A push starts background CI; it is not a stop, report, approval or waiting point. A dispatched matrix, pending or running CI/build/test, earlier failed candidate, milestone, individual blocker or routine error is never a reason to end the turn while independent authorized work remains. Inspect available results without blocking and repair confirmed failures. Do not use scheduled reminders, polling loops, sleeps, offers to continue or a final chat response as a substitute for the next actionable tool call. Before ending a turn, exhaust independent authorized coding, focused tests, security/debloat and reconciliation; wait for final required CI only when no independent work remains. Verify all mandatory checks on the identical final SHA; never claim unverified completion. Explicit owner WAIT/STOP/PAUSE/HOLD, required approvals, safety, budget and genuine exhaustion of authorized work take precedence.
- END-TO-END DELIVERY: finish every coding requirement of the latest `plan.md`, not only the mandatory ones. The work queue is Part 0A of PROGRESS.md: every line `- [ ] N.` without the word BLOCKED is open work, and OPEN means "not yet audited against the code", not "finished". Take the next open item, audit it against plan.md and the code, implement what is missing, test, record, commit, push, and take the next one. "Done" means no unblocked item is open, every required check passes on the same pushed SHA, and a re-audit of plan.md shows a result for each requirement; do not stop on your own judgement that work is exhausted. After the queue is empty, keep improving onboarding, accessibility and the plan's UX laws with tested changes. Never deploy, merge into main or spend without approval; Supabase staging only when authorized. The general rule is also in the user-level `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`.
- Turn endings: a message with no tool call ends the turn and stops all work. Do not end a turn with a summary that announces the next step, an offer to continue, a list of non-blocking decisions, or a decision that this is a good place to report. Put status and recommendations in the same message as the next tool call. Stop only where nothing can move without the owner, or where the blocker is deliberately protected from you. This does not override confirmation for risky or destructive actions.
- Respect explicit owner WAIT, STOP, PAUSE and HOLD, scope, security, permissions and approvals. No unauthorized merge, deployment, spending, force-push or irreversible actions.
